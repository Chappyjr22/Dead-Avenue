# Imported models

## Makarov

`makarov.glb` is copied from `Chappyjr22/zombie-town-updated`,
`assets/models/weapons/cc0/makarov.glb`.

Free CC0 Guns & Explosives Pack by 3dmodelscc0:
https://3dmodelscc0.itch.io/free-cc0-guns-explosives-pack

CC0 1.0 Universal. The creator dedicated the assets to the public domain.
The original mesh hierarchy is retained; runtime seats the magazine and fits
scale/position to the existing hands. No M1911 assets are included.

## City zombie

`city-zombie.glb` is copied from `Chappyjr22/zombie-town-online`,
`public/models/boss-zombie/boss-zombie.glb`.

Derived from **Male City Zombie, Ready for use in Game Engines** by Rikindle3D:
https://opengameart.org/content/male-city-zombie-ready-for-use-in-game-engines

CC0 1.0 Universal. The source asset is dedicated to the public domain.
The previous game's GLB conversion includes PBR textures and in-place walk,
attack, hit and death clips. It contains 17,839 uploaded vertices and three
material primitives. Dead Avenue uses independently cloned skeletons with
shared geometry/textures. Phone performance needs device testing with a crowd.

CC0 summary: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Legal code: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

The unchanged `walker.glb` arm source is covered separately in the root README.
